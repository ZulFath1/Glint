# Glint v0.2.0

Glint gets a **Settings window**, **5 themes with 3 different layouts**, a
choice of where the menu opens, and its own icon. Everything from 0.1.0 is
still here: the grow-from-the-taskbar animation, instant search, the music
player and the tiny memory use.

## New

- **Settings window.** Change the theme, background, colors, columns,
  animation, music player, hotkey and more, and watch the menu change next to
  it as you go. Open it from the tray icon (**Settings…**) or the gear in the
  menu. Every change is saved to `config.toml` (your comments are kept).
- **5 themes, 3 layouts.** Pick one in **Settings → Theme**; the menu changes
  live:
  - **Tokyo Night** (the new default): a rail on the left, a greeting with a
    big clock and the date, icons on colored tiles. The rail's Apps button
    shows your most-used apps.
  - **Nord**: a Rofi-style list of your most-used apps, an icon-only pinned
    strip, and a one-line music bar. Opens in the middle of the screen.
  - **Catppuccin** (Mocha): soft and round, a pill search bar, a mauve play
    button, separate Lock / Restart / Shut down buttons.
  - **Gruvbox**: terminal style, with a monospace font, a `user@glint: ~`
    title bar with a clock, a `❯` prompt and `[ lock ] [ restart ] [ off ]`.
  - **Glint**: the original glassy look.
- **Icon tint**: draw every app icon in one color, the theme's text color.
- **Bottom or center.** The menu opens above the taskbar, or in the middle of
  the screen growing out of its own center (**Settings → Layout →
  Position**). Your choice always wins over the theme's.
- **6 columns by default** (3 to 8 in Settings).
- **An app icon** for the exe, the tray and the Settings window.

Upgrading from 0.1.0? Your `config.toml` keeps working and keeps the look you
had. Pick a theme in Settings to try the new ones.

## Performance (measured on the author's PC)

- Memory (Task Manager's "Memory" column): about 4–6 MB hidden and 6–9 MB
  open, depending on the theme (the Windows Start menu: ~17 MB)
- No timers while hidden; with music playing, Glint reacts to the player's
  events (about 30 ms of CPU a minute)
- One 1.2 MB exe, no installer

## Requirements

- Windows 11 22H2 or newer
- 64-bit (x64)

## Install

1. Download `glint.exe` below and put it in a folder that will stay.
2. Run it. SmartScreen may warn because the exe isn't code-signed: click
   **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Press the Win key.

Updating from 0.1.0: choose **Quit Glint** in the tray menu, replace the old
`glint.exe` with the new one, and start it.

To uninstall: turn off **Start with Windows** if you enabled it, choose
**Quit Glint** in the tray menu, then delete the exe, `%APPDATA%\Glint` and
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Glint`.

## Known limitations

- Clicking the taskbar's Start button still opens the Windows Start menu
  (you can hide that button with a Windhawk taskbar mod).
- Search covers apps only.
- Tested on one PC so far: multi-monitor setups, other display scales, a
  taskbar on the top or side, and real CJK IMEs have had little testing.
- The exe is not code-signed.
